🧣 Winter Packing Checklist

  • 🧢 Woollen cap or beanie
  • 🧥 Warm jacket or sweater (Ooty nights can get quite cold)
  • 🧤 Gloves and a scarf
  • 🧦 Woollen socks and closed shoes
  • ☂️ Umbrella or light raincoat (Ooty gets sudden rain)
  • 🕶️ Sunglasses and sunscreen (days can be sunny)
  • 💊 Basic medicines and first-aid kit
  • 🪪 Tickets, IDs and printed copies of bookings
  • 🔦 Torch and power bank
Pack a warm layer in your hand bag for the train journey, since the morning start can feel chilly.
